    Read at a steady pace. The bar fills to the target time for this passage.

    1. Stand up and hold the phone about a hand's width from your mouth.
    2. Press record, read the whole passage in the manner the form calls for, then stop.
    3. Nothing leaves the device. Turn off any headset noise cancelling first: it strips the low end.

    Loudness is frame RMS, pitch comes from autocorrelation, and syllables from peaks in the energy envelope. Checked against Praat on real recordings, pitch lands within about 3%; speech detection, pause counts and volume contrast match a reference implementation exactly. The browser's automatic gain control and noise suppression are switched off during recording, because both would corrupt the measurements. Loud non-speech counts as speech, so record somewhere quiet: a cough or a door will widen your volume contrast. Target ranges come from a corpus of recorded speeches measured with the same method — see BENCHMARKS.md and ORATORY.md.
